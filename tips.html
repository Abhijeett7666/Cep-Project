Reducing Single-Use Plastic #1

Carry a durable cloth or canvas tote bag whenever you step out to avoid plastic bags.

Verified Habit
Reducing Single-Use Plastic #2

Bring your own stainless steel water bottle and skip buying single-use bottled water.

Verified Habit
Reducing Single-Use Plastic #3

Decline single-use plastic cutlery and straws; carry reusable portable utensils in your bag.

Verified Habit
Saving Water #4

Turn off running taps while brushing your teeth or washing hands to save up to 6 liters per minute.

Verified Habit
Saving Water #5

Report and immediately repair leaky faucets and flush valves across college hostels and campus labs.

Verified Habit
Saving Water #6

Reuse water drained from rinsing rice, pulses, and vegetables to nourish potted garden plants.

Verified Habit
Conserving Electricity #7

Switch off lights, fans, computer displays, and projectors whenever stepping out of classrooms.

Verified Habit
Conserving Electricity #8

Unplug chargers and idle electronic adapters; phantom standby power can consume 10% of household electricity.

Verified Habit
Conserving Electricity #9

Utilize natural window daylight in study spaces during daytime hours rather than artificial illumination.

Verified Habit
Recycling & Reusing #10

Use blank reverse sides of printed handouts and assignments for rough calculations and brainstorming notes.

Verified Habit
Recycling & Reusing #11

Wash and repurpose glass containers and plastic jars to organize stationery, cables, and dry groceries.

Verified Habit
Recycling & Reusing #12

Donate completed course textbooks, lab notebooks, and unused stationery to juniors or library donation boxes.

Verified Habit
Keeping Public Places Clean #13

Never litter in public streets or campus pathways; keep trash in your pocket until you spot an appropriate bin.

Verified Habit
Keeping Public Places Clean #14

Join or coordinate monthly cleanliness and plogging drives in campus grounds and neighborhood parks.

Verified Habit
Keeping Public Places Clean #15

Practice waste segregation at source by depositing waste into distinct wet and dry bins at public food courts.

Verified Habit
Organic Composting #16

Set up a terracotta pot composter for fruit and vegetable peels to produce natural nutrient-rich plant fertilizer.

Verified Habit
Organic Composting #17

Balance wet green kitchen waste with dry browns (dry leaves, shredded unprinted cardboard) to ensure odorless composting.

Verified Habit
E-Waste Disposal #18

Dispose of depleted batteries, chargers, and dead gadgets at authorized e-waste bins rather than household trash.

Verified Habit
E-Waste Disposal #19

Repair cracked screens or battery issues before deciding to replace electronic devices with new models.
