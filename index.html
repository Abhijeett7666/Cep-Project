Seamless Workflow

How The System Operates

A closed-loop civic accountability mechanism from citizen reporting to municipal resolution.

1
Citizen Reports with Evidence

A resident logs in, specifies the location and ward, selects the waste category, attaches a photo, and submits a complaint.

2
Admin Triages & Assigns

Municipal corporation officers review complaints in real-time, prioritize urgency, and dispatch designated ward sanitation workers.

3
Resolution & Audit Trail

Field teams clear the waste, administrators mark the issue as Resolved with notes, and the citizen receives transparent live updates.
