Waste Segregation Master Guide

Learn how to sort waste at source to prevent landfill contamination.

Municipal Regulation: Disposal schedules and recycling capabilities vary across municipal wards. Always adhere to local green/blue/red bin guidelines.
1. Wet Waste (Biodegradable)

Food scraps, vegetable and fruit peels, tea leaves, stale cooked food, eggshells, and garden clippings.

Bin: Green Covered Bin
Handling: Drain excess water; ideal for home or community composting.
2. Dry Recyclable Waste

Paper, notebooks, cardboard, rinsed beverage containers, metal cans, glass jars.

Bin: Blue Bin
Handling: Rinse and dry containers; flatten cartons to save space.
3. E-Waste (Electronic Waste)

Old mobile phones, chargers, dead batteries, laptop peripherals, and bulbs.

Bin: Dedicated Yellow E-Bin / Drop Kiosks
Handling: Never dispose in general trash; contains recoverable copper and toxic lead.
4. Hazardous Household Waste

Paints, thinners, chemical pesticides, concentrated drain cleaners, vehicle oil.

Bin: Red Hazardous Center Drop-off
Handling: Retain in original labeled container; NEVER pour down storm drains.
5. Special Waste (Sanitary & Bulky)

Sanitary pads, diapers, medical swabs, discarded furniture, renovation rubble.

Handling: Wrap hygiene items in newspaper with a distinct red dot. NEVER flush down toilets.
